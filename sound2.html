    • Tilt the phone from side to side to blow a brass instrument.

    Side-to-side tilt is a continuous gesture, so it controls breath pressure instead of triggering a one-shot sound. Lying flat is no breath and silence. Tilting further is like blowing harder into a horn. The model only speaks with a playable tube length and lip tension, so those parameters are set when the sound loads.
